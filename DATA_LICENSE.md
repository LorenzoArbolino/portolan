# Data licence

The Portolan database (everything under `data/`, the build outputs in `build/` and any release dump made from them) is made available under the **Open Database License (ODbL) v1.0**: https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/

Individual contents of the database are licensed under the **Database Contents License (DbCL) v1.0**: https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/dbcl/1-0/

## In short (not a substitute for the licence text)

- **Share:** you may copy, distribute and use the database.
- **Create:** you may produce works from the database.
- **Adapt:** you may modify, transform and build upon the database.

As long as you:

- **Attribute:** credit "Portolan contributors" and link to this repository, and keep `ATTRIBUTION.md` with any copy.
- **Share-alike:** if you publicly use an adapted version of the database, or works produced from an adapted database, you must also offer that adapted database under the ODbL.
- **Keep open:** if you redistribute the database, or an adapted version of it, you may use technological measures that restrict the work (such as DRM) only if you also distribute a version without such measures.

## What is not covered

- **Code** in this repository is under Apache-2.0 (`LICENSE`).
- **The GEM base layer** in `external/gem/` is a separate dataset under its own licence (CC BY-NC-SA, see the `LICENSE` file there). It is never merged into the ODbL tables, metrics or downloads.
- **Quotes** in `evidence` records are short excerpts from third-party documents, kept to cite the source of each value. Copyright in the quoted documents stays with their owners; Portolan publishes the URL, a quote of at most 400 characters and an archive link, never the document itself.
